SquadBill · Last updated 12 August 2026

Who we are

SquadBill is a mobile application operated by Brain Attic LLC, a company registered in Wyoming, United States.

SquadBill helps groups of friends organise activities and split shared costs. You can create groups, plan events, chat, scan receipts, split bills, and keep track of who has paid whom.

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what rights you have. It applies to the SquadBill mobile app and to the SquadBill web application.

Contact: Brain Attic LLC 30 N Gould St, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States Email: pyae@brainattic.dev

A short summary

  • We collect the information you give us when you sign up, the content you create in the app, and basic technical information about your device.
  • We collect financial information — your bank account or PromptPay details — so that people who owe you money can pay you directly.
  • We do not process, hold, or move money. SquadBill records who owes what. Payments happen between you and other people, using your own banking apps.
  • Receipt images are sent to Google and OpenAI for text extraction, so we can split bills automatically. You can enter bills manually instead.
  • We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell your data. We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Receipt and payment slip images are deleted 90 days after a bill is settled.
  • You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app.
  • SquadBill is for adults. You must be 18 or over to use it.

3. Automated processing and AI

SquadBill uses artificial intelligence to read receipts.

How it works. When you photograph a receipt, the image is sent to Google (Gemini) and OpenAI (GPT-4o), which extract the text — item names, prices, totals, tax. We use two providers because Thai-language receipts are difficult, and comparing two results is more accurate than trusting one.

What they receive. The receipt image itself. If your receipt shows a restaurant name, address, date, or items, those are included. We do not send your name, email, bank details, or any other account information.

What we do with the result. The extracted text is used to create the bill. You can edit anything before sending it.

If you'd rather not. Use manual bill entry instead. Everything in SquadBill works without AI scanning.

No automated decisions about you. We do not use AI or automated processing to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

4.2 Other users — including your payment details

This is the most important disclosure in this policy, so we want to be direct about it.

What other group members can see: your display name, avatar, Friend ID, the messages and photos you send, your RSVPs, and your share of any bill you're part of.

What people who owe you money can see: if you have added payment details to your profile, your PromptPay ID and bank account information are shown to members of your group who currently owe you money for a shared bill. This is the point of the feature — it lets them transfer payment to you directly.

Who cannot see them: people you share no group with; people who owe you nothing; anyone using search or browsing profiles. Your payment details are not part of your public profile.

You are in control. You can add or remove your payment details at any time under Me → Settings. Removing them does not change any bill that has already been recorded.

SquadBill does not handle money. We record who owes what, and we let people confirm when a payment has arrived. Every payment is made directly between users, through their own banking or payment applications, entirely outside SquadBill. We are not a payment service, we never hold funds, and we take no fee from any transfer between users.

6. How long we keep it

Your account. For as long as your account exists.

Bills, expenses, and payment records. For as long as your account exists. These are shared records — other members of a group rely on them for an accurate history of who paid what.

Messages and photos in chats. For as long as the group exists, or until deleted.

Receipt and payment slip images. Deleted 90 days after the associated bill is settled. We keep them for that period so that group members can refer back to a receipt or check a payment if a question arises, and no longer.

Server logs. Typically up to 90 days.

When you delete your account. We delete your personal data from our active systems. Some information may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period before being overwritten. We may retain limited records where legally required, or to resolve a dispute.

A note on shared records. If you delete your account, bills you were part of may remain visible to other group members with your name removed, so that their own records stay accurate.

7. How we protect it

  • All connections between the app and our servers use HTTPS encryption.
  • Passwords are stored using bcrypt hashing and cannot be read by us or recovered.
  • Access to production systems is restricted and protected by two-factor authentication.
  • Requests are rate-limited to reduce the risk of brute-force attacks.
  • We review our systems for security problems and fix them promptly.

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law — including, where applicable, notification to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee within 72 hours.
