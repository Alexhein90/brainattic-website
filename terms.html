3. AI features

SquadBill uses AI for two things only, and only with your permission. Nothing else you do in SquadBill is sent to AI services. More detail is in our Privacy Policy.

  • Reading receipts. When you scan a receipt, the photo is sent to Google (Gemini) and OpenAI to read the items and prices. The app asks your permission first, when you sign up or before your first scan.
  • Checking payment slips (Pro). If you allow it, payment slip photos you upload are sent to Google (Gemini) and OpenAI to read the amount and check the transfer. The app asks when you sign up, or the first time you upload a slip. A slip can show names, bank names, and account numbers.
  • You can turn either feature off at any time in Me.
  • AI can make mistakes. Review the result before you send a bill or confirm a payment.
  • Only scan receipts you are comfortable sharing with your group.
  • You can always enter a bill by hand, and slips can always be uploaded without AI.
