Reporting a message

Most of the time a SquadBill group is just friends working out who is playing on Tuesday. Now and then it isn't. If someone sends something abusive, misleading or out of place, you can report it in a couple of taps — and they are never told it was you.

  • Report a message — press and hold it, tap the flag, pick a reason, send. That's it.
  • Block someone — open their profile from Contacts and tap Block. They can't message you, and their messages stop showing up for you. You can unblock from the same place whenever you like.

We look at every report within 24 hours and remove content or accounts that don't belong here. Prefer email? Write to pyae@brainattic.dev with the group name and roughly when it happened.

Blocking and your bills

Blocking is about people, not money. Anything you already share stays exactly where it is — you can still open the bill, still settle up, still get your reminders. Blocking only stops the messages.

Common questions

I forgot my password.
Tap “Forgot password?” on the login screen and we'll email you a reset code.

How do I delete my account?
Open the Me tab, scroll to the bottom, choose Delete Account. Your profile and personal details go with it. Bills you shared stay visible to the people you shared them with, so nobody's records break.

The receipt scan got something wrong.
Every item, price and share can be edited before the bill is approved. If a particular receipt trips it up every time, send it to us — that's how the scanner gets better.

Something else?
Email pyae@brainattic.dev. Tell us your username and what you were doing when it went wrong, and we'll find it faster.
