Reporting a message or a person

SquadBill is a place to sort out money with people you know. If someone sends something abusive, misleading or inappropriate, you can report it from inside the app.

  • Report a message — press and hold the message, tap the flag, choose a reason, and send. The person is not told that you reported them.
  • Block someone — open their profile from Contacts and tap Block. They can no longer message you, and you stop seeing their messages. Unblock at any time from the same screen.

We review every report within 24 hours and remove content or accounts that break our rules. If you would rather report something by email, write to pyae@brainattic.dev with the group name and roughly when it happened.

Blocking and your money

Blocking is social, not financial. Bills you already share stay exactly as they are — you can still see them, still settle them, and still get reminders about what you owe. Blocking only stops the messages.

Common questions

I forgot my password.
Tap “Forgot password?” on the login screen. We email you a reset code.

How do I delete my account?
Open the Me tab, scroll to the bottom, and choose Delete Account. This removes your profile and personal data. Bills you shared with other people remain visible to them so their records stay accurate.

A scanned receipt came out wrong.
You can edit any item, price or split before the bill is approved. If the scan is consistently wrong on a particular receipt, email it to us and we will use it to improve the scanner.

Something else?
Email pyae@brainattic.dev. Tell us your username and what you were doing when it went wrong — it helps us find the problem faster.
